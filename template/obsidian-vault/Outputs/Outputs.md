# Outputs

Deliverables headed out of the vault, usually as PDF, sometimes as narrated audio.

Obsidian-only syntax does not survive the trip: strip `[[wikilinks]]`, `![[embeds]]` and callouts from anything with `format: pdf` or `format: audio`, and cite sources in full — the reader will not have the vault.

Add every new note to the list below — this map is maintained by hand so the vault works with no community plugins installed. [[Dataview Queries]] mirrors it for anyone who has Dataview.
